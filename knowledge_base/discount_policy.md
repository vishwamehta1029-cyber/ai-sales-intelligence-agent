# Discount Approval Policy

- Discounts up to 10% can be approved directly by the Account Executive.
- Discounts between 10% and 20% require Regional Sales Director sign-off.
- Discounts above 20% require VP of Sales and Finance approval, and must include a business justification tied to competitive displacement or strategic account expansion.
- EMEA deals closed in Q1 are eligible for an additional 3% promotional discount as part of the regional growth incentive program.
