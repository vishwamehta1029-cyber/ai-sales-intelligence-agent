# Territory Definitions

- AMER: United States, Canada, and Latin America. Segmented into Enterprise (>2,000 employees) and Commercial (<2,000 employees).
- EMEA: Europe, Middle East, and Africa, managed as a single region with country-level account ownership.
- APAC: Asia-Pacific and Japan, split into Greater China and Rest-of-APAC sub-regions for reporting purposes.
- Named accounts above $5M in annual contract value are managed by the Strategic Accounts team regardless of geography.
