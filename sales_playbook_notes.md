# Sales Playbook Notes

- Opportunities sitting in "Open - Negotiation" for more than 45 days
  should be flagged to the Regional Sales Director for a deal review.
- Win rate is tracked at the region and account-segment level; a
  region trending below 30% win rate triggers a pipeline quality review.
- Closed-lost reasons should always be logged in the CRM to support
  quarterly loss-analysis reporting to sales leadership.
